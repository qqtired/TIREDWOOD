**Source:** Animal Pack Vol.2, uploaded by the author as "Animated Animales Low Poly".
https://opengameart.org/content/animated-animales-low-poly

The source page explicitly identifies the author, license, Cat and Dog models and downloadable archive. The files used are `FBX/Cat.fbx` and `FBX/Dog.fbx`. No paid, account-only or unlicensed assets are included.

Project adaptations: FBX imported through Three.js and exported to local GLB; original skin, rig and Idle/Walking animations retained; weights normalized after the importer's four-influence limit; materials consolidated into vertex colors and recolored; small separately colored eyes added and bound to the head joint; meter scale and forward-axis wrapper added. Runtime coat variations, rest poses and animation blending are project code. Models are not represented as newly authored from scratch.

`provenance.json` records source and converted-file hashes, triangle counts and animation names. `tools/critters/convert-quaternius.mjs` reproduces conversion from the original extracted archive.

The previously investigated Ultimate Animated Animal Pack (2021) is not used: it contains no cat and its dog download was quota-limited. No assets from that pack are redistributed here.

Procedural gull/crab models are newly authored project geometry and rigs, not redistributed source photos. Anatomy references consulted: Cornell Lab of Ornithology, Ring-billed Gull identification (https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Ring-billed_Gull/id), and MarLIN, common shore crab Carcinus maenas (https://www.marlin.ac.uk/species/detail/1497). No reference photographs are bundled.
