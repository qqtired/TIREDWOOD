# Army of Ruin: Research Report

**Scope note on the title.** Searches for "Lost Native" returned nothing. Every source identifies the game as *Army of Ruin* by **Milkstone Studios** (Spain), released **June 8, 2023** on PC. The developer, publisher and release year match the brief, so this report covers that game. If "Lost Native" refers to a different title, tell me and I will re-scope.

**Research limits.** The Steam guide "Reference Guide - Army of Ruin" returned HTTP 429, so its stage, enemy and weapon data is not included. Enemy types per stage, boss names, weapon level caps and the camera setup are not in any source I could reach. These gaps are flagged in the relevant sections.

---

## 1. Game overview

- **Developer / publisher:** Milkstone Studios (both). Source: [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1918040/Army_of_Ruin/), [The Outer Haven review](https://theouterhaven.net/army-of-ruin-review).
- **Release:** June 8, 2023. Early Access began October 27, 2022. Source: Steam store page.
- **Price:** $7.99 base. Soundtrack DLC $3.99. Soundtrack bundle $10.78. Source: Steam store page.
- **Platforms:** Steam lists Windows and macOS. The Outer Haven lists Switch, PS4, PS5 and Xbox One as well. Sources: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1918040/Army_of_Ruin/), [Outer Haven](https://theouterhaven.net/army-of-ruin-review), [Nintendo store](https://www.nintendo.com/store/products/army-of-ruin-switch).
- **Official description:** "an auto-shooter in which you guide legendary heroes on a quest to defeat the Armies of Evil." Tags: Action Roguelike, Bullet Hell, Top-Down Shooter, Survivor-like.
- **Steam reception:** Very Positive, 88% of 1,370 English user reviews (Steam store page snapshot).

## 2. Game modes, stages, difficulty

- **Maps:** Five maps. Named stages include Defiled Graveyard I, II and III. Source: [Outer Haven](https://theouterhaven.net/army-of-ruin-review).
- **Run durations:** Each map offers survival for **10, 15 or 20 minutes**, plus **Endless** mode. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Ruin levels (difficulty):** Five. Higher Ruin levels add more enemies, new enemy waves, elites and bosses that retaliate when slain, and reduce EXP earned. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Achievement naming** confirms the Ruin structure per stage, e.g. "Forest I - Ruin 1 - Defeat all special bosses with Saul" and "Graveyard I - Ruin 1 - Complete the Stage with Viking Helm." Source: search results pointing to Steam global achievements.
- **Stars:** 50 total. Stage stars are tied to goals such as upgrading a weapon to Level 4 or reaching Experience Level 20 with Ariana. Each endless mode awards one star for surviving **30 minutes**. Source: Outer Haven.

## 3. Run structure, waves and bosses

- Runs are timed (10/15/20 minutes) or Endless. Source: Outer Haven.
- Bosses are called **Army Generals** in the Steam description. Early Access wave tables and boss timings are not published in any source I could access.
- **Elites and bosses:** higher Ruin levels make them retaliate on death. Some trinkets reduce boss and enemy health in a stage. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Enemy types per stage:** not identified. The Outer Haven review does not list individual enemy types.
- **Enemy variety criticism:** reviewers say enemies are largely palette swaps of each other. Source: Outer Haven; [Vaporlens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/1918040/army_of_ruin).

## 4. Characters

The Milkstone Early Access blog (October 2022) lists six playable characters. The number later grew to **24 unlockable characters** per The Outer Haven. Sources: [Milkstone blog](https://www.milkstonestudios.com/2022/10/presenting-saul-ariana/), Outer Haven.

| Character | Class | Starting weapon | Passive | Active |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saul | Wizard | Magic Staff | -1% attack cooldown per trinket level (scales with equipped trinkets) | Magic Missiles: 8 pellets around him |
| Ariana | Warrior | Quicksilver Spears | +10% attack damage every 5 levels | Shell: invulnerable for 6 s, damages enemies she touches; long cooldown |

- Saul and Ariana are the two starters, unlocked at launch. Milkstone describes them as well balanced. Later characters are described as more unbalanced and suited to specific strategies.
- Four further characters unlock by meeting conditions (not described in the blog).
- Players pick a character before each mission.
- Unlocked characters reuse the starter models in different colors. Source: Outer Haven.
- Player consensus (Steam discussion) ranks Ariana highly, since her pierce projectiles and evolutions can clear Forge Ruin 5. Opinion only.

## 5. Weapons

- **Slots:** six weapon slots and six trinket slots. Sources: Outer Haven, Steam discussion.
- **Total content:** 58 base weapons, 116 counting evolution forms. Source: Outer Haven (note: the review's "over 300 unlockables" figure does not match its itemized count of roughly 236).
- **Examples and mechanics** (Outer Haven):
  - Chain Lightning: hits multiple nearby enemies. Evolves into Lightning Storm.
  - Blood Wings: fiery waves in two diagonal directions.
  - Crawling Root: projectiles in a cone ahead.
  - Ranger Bow: fires only to the right, a fixed direction.
  - Storm Javelin: damaging ground zones.
  - Toxic Stench: damaging radius around the player.
  - Spectral Dagger: evolution requires one Wind and one Dark infusion.
  - Fire Mirror (per a player post): 45 damage every 15 seconds.
  - Explosive Flask (per a player post): about 15 dps per enemy hit at best.
  - Wolf's Fang: AoE, called the strongest weapon by one commenter.
- **Level caps:** not confirmed by any accessible source. The Steam guide would have it, but was blocked.

**Community tier opinions** (not official balance data), from a Steam discussion thread:
- Winners named: Ancestral Razor, Energy Bolt ("really the MVP"), Quicksilver Spears, Whirlwind Pickaxe, Spectral Dagger, Ranger Bow, Magic Staff.
- Losers named: Fire Mirror, Explosive Flask, Frozen Cross, Magic Needle, Wolf's Fang (disputed by others), Toxic Stench, Storm Javelin, Landslides, Meteorite Blast, Blaze.
- Developer WaaghMan's replies: "currently damage is king," so survival trinkets can starve XP; Blaze's fire rate is being increased; damage reports for trinkets and skills are planned.

## 6. Upgrades, trinkets, charms

- **Trinkets (passive items):** Dragon Heart (+10/20/30/40/50% damage by level), Ice Mirror (occasionally freezes all enemies), Magnifier (+weapon size), Soul Container (revive on death), Pearl of Pain (damages all on-screen enemies after a set time). Source: Outer Haven.
- **Elemental infusions:** Fire, Ice, Nature, Light, Darkness, Wind. Trinkets provide infusions. Weapons evolve when the required infusions are present. Chain Lightning needs one Ice and one Light infusion. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Charms (trade-offs, usually three infusions):** Growth (+30% attack size, -10% movement speed); Curiosity (+25% XP, -25% pickup range). Source: Outer Haven.
- **Evolution mechanic:** unlock the feature via Five Stars, raise the base weapon to Level 5, equip the matching trinkets, then open a chest. The next chest evolves a qualifying weapon. If several qualify, the chest picks one at random. Source: [Steam guide thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1918040/discussions/0/3819659084182811925/) and related discussions (search snippets).
- **Level-up choices:** players choose skills between missions, and re-rolls are available (15 re-rolls via permanent upgrades). Sources: Steam store page, Outer Haven.

## 7. Active abilities

- Characters have a passive and a cooldown-based active skill. Examples: Saul's Magic Missiles, Ariana's Shell. Source: Milkstone blog.
- No separate dash or ultimate is documented in the sources.

## 8. Pickups, destructibles, map objects

- **Gold** comes from crates, piggy banks and treasure chests. Higher difficulty yields more gold. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Experience gems** drop from kills; the player collects them by walking. Source: Outer Haven.
- Chests also drive weapon evolution (see section 6).
- Shrines and breakable props: not documented in the sources reached.

## 9. Map design

- **Bounded or infinite:** the sources describe five fixed maps, so arenas are finite, not infinite-scroll. Specific boundary behavior is not documented.
- **Repetition:** reviewers call the five maps the main weakness. Source: Outer Haven.

## 10. Meta progression

- **Currency:** Gold. Earned in runs and spent on permanent upgrades. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Upgrades tab:** permanent upgrades for unlocked characters: damage, fire rate, weapon size, projectile speed, XP gain, gold gain, run speed, pickup range, max health and health regeneration. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Caps:** most upgrades cap around 10 levels; re-rolls cap at 15; damage has no cap. Costs rise each purchase. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Favorites:** marking a weapon or trinket as a favorite raises its drop chance in runs, at a gold cost. Extra lives beyond Soul Container also cost gold. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Unlocks:** challenges unlock characters, weapons and other content, which unlock further challenges. Over 550 challenges, more than 200 tied to achievements. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Completion:** the reviewer estimated 60 to 70 hours for 100%. Source: Outer Haven.
- **Shop:** upgrades tab acts as the shop. No separate talent tree was found.

## 11. Comparison with Vampire Survivors

- **Genre:** both are swarm survivors with auto-fire weapons, XP gems and level-up choices. Army of Ruin adds a roguelite meta with trinket/charm systems and elemental evolutions. Sources: Outer Haven, Vaporlens.
- **Camera and art:** the sources do not describe the camera. The Outer Haven calls the graphics "well-done" with "nice, hi-res maps and enemies." The brief's "3D top-down" description is not confirmed in the sources reached.
- **Controls:** auto-fire; the player moves and collects gems. Weapon facing matters (Ranger Bow fires only right). Source: Outer Haven.
- **Reviewer comparisons:** one reviewer says "I hate to call it a Vampire Survivors clone," but it plays similarly. Another says some players see it as more modern and polished. Sources: Outer Haven, [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/1918040/army_of_ruin).
- **Vampire Survivors benchmark:** the game has over 200,000 Steam reviews at 98% positive. Source: [PCGamesN steam reviews article](https://www.pcgamesn.com/vampire-survivors/steam-reviews).

## 12. Reviewer feedback on feel, pacing, visuals

**Praise**
- Gameplay feels "very fresh and crisp." Outer Haven, score **4.5**.
- Weapons feel distinct, with little overlap. Outer Haven.
- Soundtrack "slaps" and stays in the head. Outer Haven.
- Visuals are colorful and cohesive. Vaporlens.
- Wide variety of characters, weapons and items; many challenges and unlocks. Vaporlens.
- Stable on Steam Deck and Linux/Proton. Vaporlens.
- Suits all skill levels. Outer Haven.

**Criticism**
- Five maps feel repetitive. Outer Haven, Vaporlens.
- Enemies are palette swaps. Outer Haven.
- Characters reuse the same models in different colors. Outer Haven.
- Music is "a bit on the sparse side." Outer Haven.
- Grind and slow progression, with expensive upgrades and a weak gold economy. Vaporlens.
- Early game is slow; difficulty spikes too quickly. Vaporlens.
- Weapon evolutions feel like simple strength boosts. Vaporlens.
- Some weapons are clearly stronger; the reviewer names a fire wizard as near-mandatory. Outer Haven.
- Some enemy colors blend into backgrounds. Vaporlens.
- Targeting problems reported. Vaporlens.
